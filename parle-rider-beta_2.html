What a Parle grant allows.

Rides in this beta are operated on owner-connected Teslas. Access is time-boxed: when a ride ends, the grant is revoked and the vehicle is released.

Temporary command access — wake, lock/unlock, Ready to Drive, status and End Ride, only while your grant is active.
No raw Tesla secrets on this device. Commands route through the Parle API, which checks authorization before calling Tesla.
Location while in use — required to match you to a vehicle and to open a ride, including in the background during a trip.
Owner retains authority. Availability, permissions and expiry are set by the vehicle's owner.

By continuing you agree to the Parle Rider Terms and Privacy Notice, confirm you are at least 18 and hold a valid driver's licence, and accept that operating a shared vehicle is your responsibility. Beta software; no real vehicle is contacted by this build.
